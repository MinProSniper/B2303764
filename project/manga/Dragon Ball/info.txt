Bulma, a confident and tenacious teenager, is on a mission to acquire the legendary Dragon Balls - a set of seven magical spheres capable of granting any wish. Driven by her desire for the perfect boyfriend, she crosses paths with Son Goku, a solitary figure in possession of one of the Dragon Balls passed down from his late grandfather. After convincing him to part with it, Bulma proposes that Goku joins her in her quest for the remaining Dragon Balls. As they journey together through uncharted territory, Goku's world expands beyond his wildest dreams. Along the way, they form new alliances and confront malicious enemies also in pursuit of the Dragon Balls. As Goku's love for his companions intensifies, he must dig deep and unlock an inexplicable power to safeguard them. With time, Goku's martial arts abilities improve, but his foes grow more dangerous. He uncovers the source of his immense strength, realizing that it could place his loved ones in peril. Devoting himself to training and combat, Goku fights against nefarious villains with sinister plans that threaten the world's destiny. His unwavering determination to protect his loved ones and the planet drives him forward.

Author: Akira Toriyama
